How Information Is Checked

Every page draws on one shared set of checked facts, built in five steps.

  1. Checked on Parimatch first. Bonus sizes, the sign-up form, account currencies and current promotions are taken from what Parimatch shows to players in India.
  2. Cross-checked across sources. Details shown only after sign-up, such as wagering rules and payout times, are compared across independent reviews. Where most sources agree, the value is used as stated.
  3. Hedged when sources differ. Values that vary, such as deposit maximums or the Android APK size, are given as “around” or “usually”, with a reminder that limits may vary by account.
  4. Left out when unconfirmed. Details that cannot be confirmed or change too often, such as specific promo codes or the Android app version number, are not published.
  5. Dated on every page. Pages are revised when offers or rules change, and each page shows the date of the latest update.

Current rules on Parimatch take priority. Bonus terms, limits and payout times can change without notice. Check the conditions shown in the account before making a deposit.
