Indian Favourites: Andar Bahar, Teen Patti, Hindi Roulette, Dragon Tiger

These tables are built around card games familiar to most Indian players, with short rounds and simple rules.

Card game

Andar Bahar

The dealer opens a joker card, then deals cards one by one to the Andar and Bahar sides. The bet is on which side receives the first card of the same rank. Several versions run in the lobby, including Parimatch Andar Bahar.

Card game

Teen Patti

A three-card game often called Indian poker. Hands rank from trail (three of a kind) through pure sequence, sequence, colour and pair down to high card, and the stronger hand takes the round.

Roulette

Hindi Roulette

Classic live roulette with a Hindi-speaking dealer. Bets cover single numbers, splits, red or black, odd or even, dozens and columns, and the dealer calls the result in Hindi.

Card game

Dragon Tiger

One card goes to Dragon, one to Tiger, and the higher card wins. A Tie bet covers equal ranks. With a single card per side, rounds are over very quickly.

Payouts and side bets differ between versions of the same game, so open the rules panel before the first round.
