Requirements Before the First Withdrawal

A payout request is normally approved only when five conditions are met. Checking each one before pressing Withdraw saves a support chat later.

Check 1

KYC Completed

The account must pass verification with Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence or Voter ID.

Check 2

Phone and E-Mail Confirmed

Unconfirmed contacts in Personal Data can hold a request back.

Check 3

Same Method, Own Name

Withdraw to the method used for the deposit where that method supports payouts, and only to an account registered in the player's name.

Check 4

Deposit Wagered 1x

Each deposit usually has to be staked at least once before cashing out. A ₹2,000 deposit means bets worth at least ₹2,000 in total.

Check 5

Bonus Wagering Finished

Complete the wagering on an active welcome bonus first. A request sent earlier cancels the bonus.

How to Withdraw From Parimatch in a Browser

The desktop and mobile browser versions share the same cashier, so the steps are identical on a laptop or a phone.

  1. Log in Open Parimatch in Chrome or another browser and complete Parimatch sign in with a +91 phone number, e-mail, account number, Google or Apple.
  2. Open the cashier Go to Profile or Wallet and select Withdraw.
  3. Pick a payout method Choose UPI, bank transfer or crypto. Start with the method used for the deposit if that method appears in the payout list.
  4. Enter the amount Type a sum above the method minimum and within the shown limit. Only the withdrawable balance can be cashed out, not bonus funds.
  5. Add the payout details Enter the UPI ID, the bank account number with the IFSC code, or the USDT/USDC wallet address.
  6. Confirm the request Check every detail once more and confirm with the OTP if one is sent to the registered phone.
  7. Track the status Open Transaction History to follow the request until the money arrives.

How to Withdraw in the Parimatch App

The Android APK and the iPhone web app use the same account, balance and cashier as the browser version. The flow differs slightly by method:

UPI Payout in the App

  1. Open the Parimatch app and log in.
  2. Tap Profile or Wallet, then Withdraw.
  3. Select UPI and enter the amount.
  4. Type the UPI ID linked to Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM.
  5. Confirm with the OTP and check Transaction History.

Bank or Crypto Payout in the App

  1. Open the Parimatch app and go to Withdraw.
  2. For a bank transfer, enter the account number and IFSC code.
  3. For crypto, choose USDT or USDC, the network and the wallet address.
  4. Confirm the request with the OTP if asked.
  5. Follow the status in Transaction History.

For crypto payouts, the network selected in the cashier must match the receiving wallet, and a mismatch can make the transfer hard to recover. Networks reported for USDT and USDC deposits include TRC-20, ERC-20, BEP-20, Solana and Polygon; the cashier shows which ones are available for a withdrawal. The crypto balance is held separately from the INR account.

Common Parimatch Withdrawal Problems and Fixes

KYC Still Pending

Payouts wait until verification is approved. Upload any extra document requested, such as proof of address or proof of payment method ownership.

Bonus Wagering Not Finished

Check how much turnover is left on the active bonus. Either finish the wagering or accept that an early request forfeits the bonus.

Wrong UPI ID or Bank Details

A typo in the UPI ID, account number or IFSC code blocks the transfer. Cancel the pending request and send a new one with corrected details.

Name Mismatch

The name on the UPI or bank account must match the name in Personal Data and on the KYC document.

Method Differs From Deposit

If the deposit method has no payout option, choose another method from the cashier list, such as a bank transfer in the player's own name.

Amount Outside the Limits

A sum below the method minimum is rejected. Above the UPI cap of around ₹1,00,000, split the sum into smaller requests if the cashier allows, or use a bank transfer or crypto.

The Help Centre article «Why Can't I Withdraw Funds From My Account?» covers these cases directly and can be found through the Help tab of the Online Chat widget via «Search for help». If the article does not solve the issue, a live agent replies 24/7, usually within a few minutes. More on the channels is on the Parimatch support page.

Watch out for fake support. Parimatch support does not message players first on WhatsApp and never asks for a password, OTP or UPI PIN. Share none of these to speed up a payout.

How to Cancel a Pending Parimatch Withdrawal

A withdrawal request can be cancelled while the status still shows Pending. This helps when the amount or the payout details were entered incorrectly.

  1. Open Transaction History In the browser or the app, go to Profile or Wallet and open the list of transactions.
  2. Find the pending request Look for the withdrawal marked Pending.
  3. Cancel and resend if needed Use the cancel option next to the request. The amount returns to the account balance, and a corrected request can be sent right away.

FAQ About Parimatch Withdrawal

What is the minimum withdrawal at Parimatch?

Usually ₹500–₹1,000 depending on the method, with UPI often set at ₹1,000. The cashier shows the exact minimum for each account.

How long does a Parimatch UPI withdrawal take?

Typically 15 minutes to 24 hours. The first UPI payout can take the full 24 hours, so verifying the account early helps.

Does Parimatch charge a fee for withdrawals?

No. Parimatch takes no withdrawal fee, though a bank or a crypto network may charge a fee on the receiving side.

Can I withdraw from Parimatch without verification?

No. KYC with Aadhaar, PAN or another accepted ID is required before the first payout, including any winnings from a welcome bonus.

Can I withdraw to a different UPI ID than the one used for the deposit?

The payout should go back to the deposit method whenever that method supports withdrawals, and always to an account in the player's own name. A UPI ID registered to someone else is not accepted.

What should I do if a Parimatch withdrawal is pending for too long?

Check KYC status, bonus wagering and the payout details first. If the request is still pending after 48 hours, contact the Online Chat with the transaction details from Transaction History.
