Principles of Responsible Gambling

Responsible gambling means betting on Parimatch cricket, football or casino games only with money and time set aside in advance. Every bet carries a real risk of losing the stake, and no tipster, strategy or system changes that.

Rule 1

Adults Only

Accounts are open only to players aged 18 and over.

Rule 2

Entertainment, Not Income

Treat a deposit like a cinema ticket: money paid for fun, not an investment expected to pay back.

Rule 3

A Fixed Budget

Choose a monthly amount that can be lost in full, and stop once it is spent.

Rule 4

Time Under Control

Decide how long a session lasts before it starts, especially during IPL season.

Money for betting should never come from loans, credit cards, family savings or funds meant for rent, bills and EMIs.

Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Problem gambling usually builds up slowly. The signs below deserve attention, especially when several appear together:

  • Chasing losses. Placing bigger or more frequent bets to win back money already lost.
  • Spending more than planned. Topping up again and again in one session, or going past the monthly budget.
  • Borrowing to bet. Using loans, credit, money from friends or savings meant for other goals.
  • Hiding the habit. Lying to family or friends about the time or money spent on betting.
  • Cancelling withdrawals. Reversing a pending withdrawal to keep playing with the money.
  • Betting to escape. Gambling to cope with stress, loneliness, exam pressure or problems at work.
  • Neglecting daily life. Missing work, classes, sleep or family time because of betting.

Recognised any of these signs? Set a deposit limit or take a time-out today, and consider talking to one of the help services listed below.

How to Set Limits and Self-Exclusion

Set a Deposit or Session Limit

  1. Log in to Parimatch in the browser or the Parimatch app.
  2. Open the profile and go to the account settings.
  3. Find the limit options and pick a deposit or session limit.
  4. For a deposit limit, choose daily, weekly or monthly and enter the amount in ₹; for a session limit, set the time.
  5. Save the changes and check that the new limit appears in the account.

Request a Time-Out or Self-Exclusion

  1. Open the Online Chat, available 24/7, or look for the self-exclusion option in the account settings.
  2. Ask for a time-out or self-exclusion and say whether the break should be temporary or permanent.
  3. Confirm the account with the phone number, e-mail or account number. Support never asks for a password or OTP and does not message first on WhatsApp.
  4. Ask what happens to the remaining balance and any active bonus.
  5. Wait for confirmation that the restriction is active, then delete the Parimatch app or home-screen shortcut.

Set limits at a calm moment, not in the middle of a session. If an option is missing from the settings, the Online Chat can set the limit on request; chat and Help Centre details are on the Parimatch support page.

Tips for Safer Play

  • Fix the budget before the first deposit. Set a monthly deposit limit equal to the budget before the first top-up. UPI methods and minimums are in the Parimatch deposit guide.
  • Read bonus terms first. Wagering rules, such as x16 the bonus amount within 3 days for the Sports bonus, can push towards more bets than planned. The sign-up form also allows choosing no bonus at all; full terms are on the Parimatch bonus page.
  • Never chase losses. A losing streak is a signal to stop for the day, not to raise stakes.
  • Withdraw winnings regularly. Requesting a UPI payout after a win, and leaving the request alone while pending, keeps the money from being bet away. Steps are in the Parimatch withdrawal guide.
  • Review the history every week. Bet history and Transaction History show exactly how much has gone in and out.
  • Play with a clear head. Skip betting after alcohol or when tired, angry or upset.
  • Take breaks and mute prompts. Set an alarm for the end of a session and turn off promotional push notifications if they trigger unplanned bets.

Protecting Minors

Parimatch does not accept players under 18. Every new player ticks an 18+ box in the sign-up form, and name and date of birth are checked against Aadhaar, PAN or another ID during account verification. Parents and guardians can add a few simple barriers at home:

  • Do not share Parimatch login details, passwords or OTPs with children or teenagers.
  • Log out on shared devices and do not save the password in the browser.
  • Keep the UPI PIN private, since deposits are confirmed with the UPI PIN or an OTP.
  • Remove the Parimatch app or home-screen shortcut from devices that children use.
  • Turn on the parental controls and screen-time settings built into Android and iOS.

If a minor has used an adult's account, report the situation to the Online Chat without delay.
