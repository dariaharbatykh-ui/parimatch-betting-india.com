Tennis Betting Tips: Surface, Form and Retirements

No approach removes risk, but a few habits make tennis odds easier to read.

Check the Surface

Clay slows the ball, so breaks of serve come more often. Grass and fast hard courts favour big servers: fewer breaks, more tie-breaks, which matters for total games and break-of-serve markets. Compare results on the current surface, not just the ranking.

Read Form and Schedule

Recent results say more than reputation. Look at the number of matches played in recent weeks, long-haul travel and a long match in the previous round.

Study the Matchup

Head-to-head records show how styles fit together, such as a strong returner against a big server. In doubles, settled pairs often have an edge over new partnerships.

Know the Retirement Rules

Mid-match retirements and walkovers are part of tennis. Settlement then depends on the market and the bookmaker's rules, and can differ between match winner, set and game markets. Read the rules before backing a player with a known injury, and ask the Online Chat on the support page if a settlement looks unclear.

Be Careful With Lower Tiers

Challenger and ITF form is harder to judge, so smaller stakes make sense there.

Set a Budget First

Betting is for adults 18+ and always carries a risk of losing money. Deposit limits, session reminders and time-outs sit in the account settings; the responsible gambling page covers these tools and free help.
