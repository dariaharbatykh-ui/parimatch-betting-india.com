How to Open the Parimatch Online Chat

  1. Sign in if possible. Open Parimatch in Chrome, Safari or the Parimatch app and log in, so the question is tied to the right account. Access problems are covered on the Parimatch login page.
  2. Go to Customer Support. Choose Online Chat under Chats. The FAQ entry in the same menu leads straight to the Help Centre.
  3. Search on the Home tab first. The widget opens on Home. Type a short query such as “withdrawal pending” or “app not loading” into “Search for help”; routine questions often have a ready article.
  4. Write in Messages. If no article fits, describe the problem in one or two sentences. A bot may reply first; ask for a live agent if the automated answers do not help.
  5. Share details when asked. Have the account ID, transaction ID and screenshots ready, and keep the chat open until the agent replies.

What to Prepare Before Contacting Support

Agents look up accounts and payments by ID, so a clear first message means fewer follow-up questions.

  • Account ID. The account number that also works as a login, plus the registered +91 number or e-mail.
  • Transaction ID. Taken from Transaction History. For a UPI deposit, add the UTR number from the UPI app.
  • Amount, method and time. The sum, the app used (PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay) and the exact date and time.
  • Screenshots. The error, the successful payment screen or the pending request, ready if the agent asks.
  • Device. Browser, Android APK or iOS web app.

Deposit Not Credited

  1. Wait a few minutes after the UPI payment.
  2. Enter the UTR in the cashier if a field is shown.
  3. Send the UTR, amount and payment screenshot to the chat.

Withdrawal Pending

  1. Check KYC status and active bonus wagering.
  2. Confirm the UPI ID or bank account and IFSC.
  3. Send the transaction ID from Transaction History.

How to Spot Fake Parimatch Support

Genuine support never writes first on WhatsApp and never asks for a password or OTP. A UPI PIN should never be shared with anyone. Any message asking for these details is a scam, whatever logo appears on the profile.

Common warning signs:

  • An unexpected WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS message from “Parimatch support” about a Parimatch bonus or a stuck withdrawal.
  • A request to install a screen-sharing app or read out an SMS code.
  • An offer to unlock a withdrawal for an upfront fee paid to a personal UPI ID. Parimatch charges no fees on deposits or withdrawals.
  • A link to a page that copies the Parimatch login form.

If something was already shared, stop replying, change the password through “Forgot your password?” and report the contact in the Online Chat from the account.

Limits, Time-Outs and Self-Exclusion

Support also handles responsible-gambling requests. Deposit limits (daily, weekly or monthly), session limits and reminders, and time-outs sit in the account settings, while temporary or permanent self-exclusion can be set there or requested through the Online Chat.

FAQ About Parimatch Support

Is Parimatch customer support available 24/7?

Yes. The Online Chat answers around the clock, and Help Centre articles are open at any hour.

Does Parimatch support have a WhatsApp number or phone line?

The support channels in the account are the 24/7 Online Chat and the Help Centre. Numbers shared on WhatsApp or social media cannot be verified, and genuine support never writes first on WhatsApp, so treat such messages as a scam.

How fast does Parimatch support reply?

A live agent usually replies within a few minutes. The bot or help search may answer first, and waits can be longer at busy times.

Can I talk to Parimatch support in Hindi?

Yes. The Online Chat works in English and Hindi.

What should I send support about a missing UPI deposit?

The UTR number, amount, payment app, date and time, plus a screenshot of the successful payment. Never send a password, OTP or UPI PIN.

When should I contact support about a pending withdrawal?

UPI withdrawals usually take 15 minutes to 24 hours and bank transfers 1–3 working days. If a request is still pending after 48 hours, send the transaction ID from Transaction History to the Online Chat.
