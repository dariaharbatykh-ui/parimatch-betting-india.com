General Welcome Bonus Rules

  • Welcome offers are for new players in India aged 18 and over.
  • One welcome bonus per person and per account: one of Sports, Slots, Live Casino or Instant Games, in INR, USDT or USDC.
  • The selected offer cannot be swapped for another one later.
  • Only bets in the named section count toward wagering.
  • Real money is used first, bonus funds second.
  • A withdrawal before wagering is finished cancels the bonus and any winnings made with the bonus.
  • Cashed-out, system, voided and free bets do not count toward wagering.
  • A maximum bet limit applies while a bonus is being wagered; check the exact figure in the bonus terms.
  • Verification (KYC) is required before bonus winnings can be withdrawn. Documents and timing are covered in the Parimatch verification guide.
  • Bonus abuse or multiple accounts void the bonus.

Separately from bonus wagering, the deposit itself reportedly has to be wagered at least once (x1) before a withdrawal. UPI payouts, limits and processing times are explained on the Parimatch withdrawal page.

Common Bonus Mistakes to Avoid

  • Depositing before choosing a bonus. The welcome offer attaches to the first deposit and the choice cannot be changed afterwards, so pick the offer in the sign-up form first.
  • Paying ₹300 for the Instant Games offer. A ₹300 UPI payment clears the ₹100 threshold for Sports, Slots and Live Casino, but the Instant Games bonus needs at least ₹350.
  • Treating Aviator as a slot. Aviator and Parimatch JetX sit in Instant Games, so rounds there count only toward the Instant Games bonus, not toward a Slots or Live Casino bonus.
  • Depositing more than 3 days of play can cover. A ₹10,000 deposit with the Sports bonus adds ₹15,000 and needs ₹2,40,000 in qualifying bets, roughly ₹80,000 a day. Bonus funds left unwagered after 3 days expire.
  • Cashing out during wagering. Cashing out an IPL bet early settles the bet at the current offer, but that bet then adds nothing to the wagering total.
  • Leaving verification until the end. KYC usually takes a few hours and up to 24 hours, or up to 48 hours in busy periods. Completing the Aadhaar or PAN check while wagering avoids a wait at withdrawal.
  • Registering with someone else's details. The name and date of birth must match the ID at verification, and multiple accounts void the bonus.

18+ only. A bonus adds turnover, not guaranteed profit. Set a deposit limit before chasing a wagering target, and stop if the 3-day timer pushes stakes higher than planned. Limits, time-outs and help lines are on the responsible gambling page.

FAQ About Parimatch Bonuses

Which Parimatch welcome bonus is the best choice?

The best choice depends on the section played most. The Sports bonus has the lowest wagering at x16 the bonus. The Slots bonus has the largest cap at ₹1,05,000 but needs x35 within 3 days.

Do I need a promo code for the Parimatch welcome bonus?

No. The welcome bonus is selected in the sign-up form. Campaign codes from e-mails, social media or partners go in Promotions → Promo code after login.

How long is the wagering period for a Parimatch bonus?

All four welcome bonuses must be wagered within 3 days of crediting. Bonus funds that are not wagered in time expire.

What is the minimum deposit for the Parimatch welcome bonus?

The reported qualifying minimum is ₹100 for the Sports, Slots and Live Casino offers and ₹350 for Instant Games. UPI deposits usually start at ₹300, and crypto deposits at 10 USDT/USDC.

Can bonus money be withdrawn straight away?

No. The bonus has to be wagered in full first, the account must pass verification, and winnings from the bonus are capped, for example at x5 the bonus for the Sports offer.

Can I switch to a different welcome bonus after registration?

No. Only one welcome bonus is available per person, and the choice made in the sign-up form cannot be changed later.
