How the Parimatch Affiliate Program Works

  1. The partner gets a tracking link. After joining, personal links are issued for a site, channel or campaign.
  2. The link goes into content. Typical placements are match previews, bonus overviews, app reviews, banners and social posts.
  3. A visitor signs up and plays. The new player registers, makes a first deposit and places bets or opens casino games.
  4. The account is tied to the partner. Because the sign-up came through the tracking link, the player's activity counts toward that partner's commission.
  5. Commission is calculated and paid. Earnings are worked out under the agreed model, and payouts usually arrive once a month.

Attribution depends entirely on the tracking link. Check every placement before publishing: a broken or mistyped link means the sign-up is not counted for the partner.

Parimatch Affiliate Commission Models

Partner sources report three deal types for Parimatch affiliates. RevShare is described as tiered, so the percentage depends on the tier a partner reaches, and the exact figures for each model are confirmed when the deal is agreed.

Revenue Share (RevShare)A percentage of the revenue generated by referred players, calculated for every payout period; tiered ratesContent sites and channels with a steady, returning audience

Partner sources put RevShare caps at around 40–45% on the top tiers. Treat this as a reported range, not a guaranteed rate: the figure on a specific deal may be lower and can vary by partner.

A simple rule of thumb: CPA brings income sooner, RevShare keeps paying while referred players stay active, and Hybrid mixes both.

How to Join the Parimatch Affiliate Program

  1. Prepare traffic details. Note the main traffic source (site, channel or community), the audience location and approximate monthly reach.
  2. Apply as a partner. Submit a partner application with contact details and a short description of how the traffic is generated.

  6. Launch and review results. Place the links, see which content brings sign-ups and adjust campaigns within the traffic rules below.

No joining fee. Joining is reported to be free, so treat any request for a paid “activation” or registration fee as a red flag.

Traffic Rules for Parimatch Affiliates

Exact restrictions are written in the partner agreement, but basic responsible marketing standards apply to any gambling promotion:

  • Adults only. Target players aged 18+, show an 18+ mark next to promotions and never aim content at minors.
  • No misleading claims. Do not promise winnings, guaranteed profit or risk-free betting, and do not present betting as a way to earn money.
  • Accurate bonus information. Describe offers exactly as listed, for example 150% up to ₹20,000 for sports with x16 wagering within 3 days. The welcome bonus is chosen in the sign-up form without a code, so never present a tracking code as a universal or exclusive promo code.
  • Honest sign-ups. No fake or duplicate accounts, self-referrals or registrations on behalf of other people. Each player may hold only one account, and multi-accounting voids bonuses.
  • Responsible gambling messaging. Add a short reminder to set limits and link to help resources. Parimatch offers deposit limits, session reminders, time-outs and self-exclusion, described on the responsible gambling page.
  • Local rules. Gambling and advertising rules differ between Indian states and ad networks, so check what applies to the audience and channel before a campaign goes live.

Betting always carries a risk of loss. Content that presents Parimatch as a source of income misleads readers. Keep promotions factual and point anyone who feels betting is getting out of control to the Government of India Tele-MANAS helpline on 14416.

How often are Parimatch affiliate commissions paid?

Who can affiliates target with Parimatch promotions?
