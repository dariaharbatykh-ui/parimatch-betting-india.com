Esports Betting Tips

  • Check line-ups before the start. In team games, a stand-in replacing one of five players can change how a side plays.
  • Wait for the map veto. In CS2 and Valorant, teams ban and pick maps before the series. Map winner and round totals are easier to judge once the map list is known.
  • Follow the patch. Balance updates in Dota 2 and League of Legends change which heroes and champions are strong.
  • Respect the format. A best-of-one leaves little room to recover from a slow start, so upsets are more frequent than in a best-of-three.
  • Read odds as probabilities. Dividing 1 by the decimal odds gives the implied chance of an outcome: odds of 2.50 mean 40%. If a personal estimate is lower, the price offers no value.
