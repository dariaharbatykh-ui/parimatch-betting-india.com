How the site is made and funded is explained on the page about Parimatch Betting India. The contact address for privacy questions is given at the end of this policy.

Information the Site Collects

Server Logs from the Hosting Provider

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any web host, each page request creates a technical record. Such a record may include:

  • the IP address of the device;
  • the browser and operating system (user agent);
  • the page requested, with date and time;
  • the referring page, if the browser sends one.

Cloudflare uses this data to deliver pages and to block attacks or abusive bots. Logs are not used to build reader profiles or to identify individual readers.

E-mails Sent by Readers

When a reader writes to the contact address, the message, the sender address and any details included in the text are received. This information is used only to answer the message, fix an error on a page or handle a privacy request.

What the Site Does Not Collect

The site has no registration, no log-in, no comment section, no newsletter and no contact forms. No analytics, heatmap or advertising scripts are installed, and fonts and images load from the same server, so reading a page sends no data to third-party services.

Cookies

The site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may place a strictly necessary security cookie to tell real visitors from bots. Full details, including how to block or delete cookies in a browser, are on the Cookie Policy page.

Affiliate Links and Third-Party Sites

Buttons such as “Sign Up” and “Download App” are affiliate links. A click first passes through a tracking link and then opens Parimatch in a new tab. The tracking service may record the time of the click, the IP address and the device or browser type so that a sign-up can be credited to this site.

Once Parimatch opens, everything entered there is handled by the bookmaker under a separate privacy policy and terms, not by this site. That includes phone numbers, e-mail addresses, KYC documents, deposits and bet history. The affiliate programme shares only anonymised statistics with this site, such as numbers of clicks and sign-ups, never names, contact details or documents of players.

Questions about a Parimatch account, KYC data or payments can only be answered by Parimatch support through the Online Chat, open 24/7, or the Help Centre. The Parimatch support page explains how to reach an agent.

How Information Is Shared

Personal data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. Information is passed on only in these cases:

  • to Cloudflare, as the hosting provider needed to run the site;
  • when required by law, for example in response to a valid request from a court or authority;
  • to protect the site against fraud, abuse or security threats.

Cloudflare runs a global network, so request data may be processed on servers outside India.

Data Retention

Server logs are kept by Cloudflare for a limited period under the provider's own retention rules and are not combined with other data. E-mails are kept only as long as needed to answer and to keep a short record of the correspondence, then deleted. A message can be deleted earlier on request, unless the law requires keeping the message.

Your Rights

Any reader who has sent an e-mail to the site can ask to:

  • receive a copy of the personal data held about the reader;
  • correct inaccurate details;
  • delete the correspondence;
  • withdraw consent to further contact.

Requests are answered without undue delay. To protect the data, a request may need to come from the same address used in the original message. Requests about server logs are passed on to Cloudflare, since the hosting provider holds that data.

This site cannot view, change or delete a Parimatch account. Requests about account data, KYC documents or transaction history must be made to Parimatch directly.

Security

All pages are served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The site holds very little personal data, though no online service can promise complete security.

Never send sensitive data by e-mail. The site never asks for passwords, OTP codes, UPI PINs, net banking log-ins or copies of Aadhaar or PAN. Treat any message claiming to come from this site and asking for such data as fraud.

Children and Age Limit

The site is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Content is not directed at minors, and the site does not knowingly process data of anyone under 18. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has sent an e-mail, write to the contact address and the message will be deleted.

Betting and casino games carry a real risk of losing money. Set deposit and time limits, and see the responsible gambling page for help services.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the hosting setup, tools on the site or legal requirements change. The new version is published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Use of the site is also covered by the Terms and Conditions.

Send questions about this policy and personal data requests to contact@parimatch-betting-india.com, the address also listed on the Contacts page. Write “Privacy request” in the subject line, say what is needed (a copy, a correction or deletion) and send the request from the address used in earlier correspondence.
