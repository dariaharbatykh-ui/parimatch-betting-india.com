Kabaddi Betting Markets at Parimatch

Kabaddi markets cover three things: the result, the score and individual raiders. The exact set varies by match and tournament, and can differ between pre-match and live.

Kabaddi points come from three sources: raids (touch and bonus points won by the attacker), tackles (when defenders stop the raider) and all-outs (when a whole side is put out). Raid point markets count only the points won by raiders, so the form of the lead raiders matters most.

The draw in kabaddi. In formats where a match can end level, a 1X2 bet on either team loses on a tie. Handicap and total points markets are other ways to back a view on a close game; the rules of each market show how a level score is settled.

Kabaddi Betting Tips

  • Look at attack and defence separately. A side with in-form raiders can still struggle if the defence keeps failing to stop opposing raids.
  • Check the line-up before player bets. Top raider and player raid point bets depend on the player actually taking the mat.
  • Remember the draw. Where a tie is possible, evenly matched teams can finish level, which beats both team outcomes in 1X2. Handicap and totals markets sidestep the draw.
  • Watch before betting live. The first few raids show which defence is holding up and which raider is in rhythm.
  • Follow the season context. Standings, busy match schedules and the stage of the Pro Kabaddi League can affect team selection and intensity.
  • Fix the stake in advance. Decide how much to spend per match day and do not raise stakes to win back losses.
