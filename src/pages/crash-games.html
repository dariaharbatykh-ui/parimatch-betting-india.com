What Are Crash Games at Parimatch

A crash game is a short round built around one number: a multiplier that starts climbing as soon as the round begins. The stake is placed before the start, and Cash Out pays the stake times the current multiplier. If the round crashes first, the stake is lost.

For example, ₹100 cashed out at 2.5x returns ₹250, while the same ₹100 left running past the crash returns nothing. The crash point is random, so every round comes down to one decision: when to take the money.

Auto Cash Out, Two Bets and Risk

The Aviator bet panel shows the typical controls of a crash game:

  • Auto Cash Out. The bet is collected automatically when the round reaches a chosen multiplier. No late clicks, but the win is capped at the target.
  • Auto Bet. Repeats the same stake every round until switched off, which drains a balance fast without a budget.
  • Two bets per round. For example, one stake with a low auto target and one cashed out by hand.
  • Round history and statistics. Past multipliers and the Top wins tab are visible, but past rounds do not change the next one.

An RTP of 97% means Aviator is designed to return about ₹97 per ₹100 staked over a very large number of rounds; a single session can swing far from that. The maximum bet is reported at around ₹8,000, and limits may vary by account. No fixed multiplier ceiling is published, but the payout per bet is capped by the game rules.

Aviator rounds are Provably Fair: the result comes from the server seed and the client seeds of the first three players who bet, and can be verified in the game.

No one can predict the crash point. Paid "signals" and "predictor" apps cannot know a result built from players' seeds. Treat them as scams, and never share a password or OTP.
