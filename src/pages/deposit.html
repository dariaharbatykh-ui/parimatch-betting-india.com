How to Deposit via UPI Step by Step

A UPI deposit needs only a logged-in account and a UPI app with a linked bank account.

  1. Log inSign in with a +91 phone number, e-mail or account ID and password, or with Google or Apple if the account was opened that way.
  2. Open the cashierTap «Deposit» in the top right corner next to the balance, or open Wallet.
  3. Choose UPISelect UPI or a named option such as PhonePe, Paytm or Google Pay.
  4. Enter the amountFrom ₹300. On a first deposit, check that the welcome bonus picked at sign-up is attached.
  5. Approve in the UPI appConfirm the payment with the UPI PIN, or with an OTP if the bank asks for one.
  6. Check the balanceThe money usually appears within a few minutes, and the payment is listed in Transaction History.

Paying with a UPI QR Code and UTR

  1. Select UPI QR in the cashier and enter the amount.
  2. Scan the code with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or another UPI app.
  3. Pay the same amount as entered in the cashier and approve with the UPI PIN.
  4. Open the payment details in the UPI app and copy the UTR, the transaction reference number. Depending on the app, the label reads «UTR», «UPI Ref No.» or «UPI transaction ID».
  5. If the cashier asks for the UTR, paste the number into the field and submit.

Keep the UTR. The reference number lets support match a UPI payment to the account, so save a screenshot of the payment details until the balance updates.

Deposit Not Credited: What to Check

If the balance has not changed after a few minutes, go through these checks before paying again: a second payment only creates another transaction to trace.

SituationWhat to do
UPI app shows «Pending»The bank has not completed the payment yet. Wait a few minutes and refresh the balance.
UPI app shows «Failed», but money was debitedThe bank usually reverses a failed UPI debit. Contact the bank if the money does not come back.
UPI QR payment successful, balance unchangedEnter the UTR in the cashier if a field is shown, or send the number to Online Chat.
Amount below ₹300 or 10 USDT/USDCThe payment falls outside the cashier limits. Send the payment details to Online Chat.
Crypto sent, crypto balance unchangedCheck confirmations in the wallet or a block explorer, allow about 30 minutes and make sure the same network was used.
Paid from someone else's UPI or bank accountSend the payment details to Online Chat and make future deposits only from an account in your own name. Proof of payment method ownership may be requested.

Deposit Safety Tips

Pay from Your Own Accounts

Use a UPI ID or bank account in the name used for KYC. Withdrawals go back to the deposit method where payouts are supported, and a name mismatch can hold up a cash-out.

Keep PIN and OTP Private

Support never asks for a password or OTP and does not start conversations on WhatsApp. A UPI PIN is typed only inside the UPI app.

Top Up Only in the Cashier

Start every payment from the Deposit button inside the account. Ignore UPI IDs or QR codes offered in messengers or on social media.

Set a Deposit Limit

Daily, weekly or monthly deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion are available in account settings. Details are in the responsible gambling guide.

18+ only. Betting always carries a risk of loss. Deposit only money you can afford to lose, and never try to win back losses with a new top-up.

FAQ About Parimatch Deposits

What is the minimum deposit at Parimatch in India?

₹300 via UPI, PhonePe, Paytm or Google Pay, and 10 USDT or USDC for crypto. Welcome bonuses qualify from ₹100 (₹350 for Instant Games), but the payment minimum still applies.

How long does a Parimatch deposit take?

UPI, PhonePe, Paytm and Google Pay payments are usually credited within a few minutes. Crypto takes up to about 30 minutes after network confirmation.

Does Parimatch charge a deposit fee?

No. Parimatch takes no deposit fee, but a bank, provider or blockchain network may charge one.

Is verification needed before the first deposit?

No. Deposits and bets work before KYC. Verification with Aadhaar, PAN or another ID is needed before the first withdrawal, and proof of payment method ownership may be requested; see the Parimatch KYC guide.

Where is the UTR for a UPI payment?

In the payment details inside the UPI app. The cashier may ask for this number after a UPI QR payment.

Can a deposit be withdrawn without placing bets?

Usually not. The deposit generally has to be wagered at least once (1x) before a cash-out, and KYC must be complete. Methods and timings are on the Parimatch withdrawal page.
