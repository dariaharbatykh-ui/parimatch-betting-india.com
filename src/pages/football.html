Singles, Parlays and System Bets

Football selections can be placed as a single, combined across matches in a parlay (also called an accumulator or express) or built into a system bet. Outright markets cover tournament and league winners.

Odds are shown in decimal format by default. Fractional, American, Hong Kong, Indonesian and Malaysian formats can be chosen instead.

Pick markets you understand. Double chance and draw no bet usually carry lower odds than a straight win, because they cover the draw or return the stake on it. Correct score and HT/FT pay more because the right answer is far less likely.

How Cash Out Works

Cash Out settles a bet before the final whistle for an amount based on the current odds. A partial Cash Out takes part of that amount now and leaves the rest of the stake running. The option appears on selected markets only, and the offer changes as the match goes on.

Cash Out and the bonus. Cashed-out bets do not count toward wagering of the Sports welcome bonus. With a bonus active, letting a qualifying bet run is the only way for that stake to count.
