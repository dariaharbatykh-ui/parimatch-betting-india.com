What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the browser. They can keep a session open, protect a server or record how a visitor arrived. First-party cookies come from the site being visited; third-party cookies come from another domain, such as an ad network.

Personal data in general is covered by the Privacy Policy; this page deals with cookies only.

Cookies Set by Parimatch Betting India

The site sets no cookies for advertising, analytics or personalisation, and writes nothing to local storage. Pages contain no tracking pixels, social widgets or ad scripts. Fonts and images load from the same server, not from outside services.

Because no optional cookies are set, there is nothing to opt in to before reading a guide. If an analytics or advertising tool is ever added, this policy will be updated first, and consent will be requested before such cookies are set where the law requires.

Hosting and Security Cookies

The site runs on Cloudflare, which may set strictly necessary cookies to tell real visitors from bots, block an attack or remember that a security check was passed. Typical examples are __cf_bm for bot filtering and cf_clearance after a passed check.

These cookies cannot be switched off from the site, are not used for advertising, build no reader profile and are not passed to Parimatch. Blocking security cookies in the browser is possible, but a page may then show an extra security check.

What Deleting Cookies Means for a Parimatch Account

Deleting cookies for Parimatch ends the session in that browser. The next visit needs a fresh log-in with a +91 phone number, e-mail or account number and password, or with Google or Apple for accounts opened that way. A one-time code may be requested, as on a new device.

The balance, bet history and active bonuses are stored on the account, not in the browser. Blocking all cookies, however, usually stops the Parimatch log-in from working. A gentler setup is to block only third-party cookies, or to allow cookies for the bookmaker while blocking the rest.

Clearing cookies and cache is also a common first fix when the sign-up form or log-in page does not load. If the problem stays, try the Parimatch app or another browser, or contact the Online Chat. The Parimatch login guide covers other access problems, such as OTP codes that do not arrive.

The Parimatch Android app keeps data separately from the browser, so clearing browser cookies does not sign out of the app. To reset the app, open the phone's Settings → Apps, find the Parimatch app and clear the app storage; the account is then signed out.

How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

Every browser can delete stored cookies or block new ones, either for all sites or for a single site.

Google Chrome

  1. Open the ⋮ menu → Settings → Privacy and security, on a computer or an Android phone.
  2. Open Third-party cookies and choose Block third-party cookies.
  3. Delete browsing data → Cookies and other site data removes stored cookies.

Safari

  1. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Apps → Safari (Settings → Safari before iOS 18).
  2. Tap Clear History and Website Data, or open Advanced and turn on Block All Cookies.
  3. On a Mac, use Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Choose Strict or Custom under Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  3. Under Cookies and Site Data, choose Clear Data or Manage Data.

Microsoft Edge

  1. Open the ⋯ menu → Settings and type «cookies» in the settings search box.
  2. Open Manage and delete cookies and site data and turn on Block third-party cookies.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete, tick Cookies and other site data and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly between versions, and the settings search box finds the cookie options quickly. The same Ctrl+Shift+Delete shortcut (Cmd+Shift+Delete on a Mac) opens the browsing-data dialog in Chrome and Firefox too. Private or incognito windows delete cookies when closed.
