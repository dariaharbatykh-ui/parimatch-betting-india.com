Indian Premier League

IPL fixtures get the deepest coverage on the cricket line. Beyond the match winner, a big IPL game lists top batsman and top bowler, sixes and boundaries, powerplay runs, runs in a given over and player props such as a batter to score 30+ or 50+. Outright markets cover the title winner.

The T20 World Cup, the Cricket World Cup, the World Test Championship and the Women's World Cup are covered match by match, with outrights on the eventual winner. Overseas T20 Leagues

Live Cricket Betting: Ball-by-Ball Odds and Cash Out

Next ball covers the outcome of a single delivery, next over covers the runs in the coming over, and next wicket covers how or when the next batter is dismissed. Tips for Responsible Cricket Betting

Cricket betting is entertainment for adults aged 18+, not a way to earn money, and no market guarantees a win. A long tournament and ball-by-ball markets make overspending easy, so a plan before the first bet helps.

  • Set a budget for the whole tournament. An IPL season or an ICC event runs for weeks; daily, weekly or monthly deposit limits keep spending inside that budget.
  • Slow down in live play. Short live markets invite one more bet after every delivery. Session limits and reminders show how long a session has lasted.
  • Do not chase losses. A bigger stake on the next over after a loss rarely fixes a bad day and often deepens the hole.
  • Keep loyalty out of the stake. Backing a favourite team at any price is fandom, not analysis.
  • Read the market rules. Settlement of rain-reduced or abandoned matches depends on the bet terms.
  • Take a break when needed. Time-outs and temporary or permanent self-exclusion are available through the account settings or the Online Chat.
