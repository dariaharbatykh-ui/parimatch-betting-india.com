Never send a password, OTP, UPI PIN, net banking login or copies of Aadhaar or PAN to this address. The site does not need such data and has no use for it.

KYC documents are uploaded only inside the Parimatch account, under Personal Data and Account Confirmation in the profile.

Parimatch Betting India never sends private messages first. Genuine Parimatch support does not write first on WhatsApp and never asks for a password or OTP, so treat such messages as fraud.
