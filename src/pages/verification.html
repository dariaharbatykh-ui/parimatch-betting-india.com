How to Verify a Parimatch Account Step by Step

  1. Log in. Open Parimatch in a browser or in the Parimatch app and sign in with a phone number, e-mail or account number, or with Google or Apple if the account was opened that way.
  2. Fill in Personal Data. Go to Profile → Personal Data and enter the full name, date of birth, country and city exactly as printed on the ID.
  3. Confirm the phone and e-mail. Enter the SMS code and open the e-mail link if the profile shows these contacts as unconfirmed.
  4. Open Account Confirmation. This section of the profile holds the KYC options.
  5. Choose a verification route. Enter the Aadhaar number and the SMS code, or upload a photo of Aadhaar or PAN.
  6. Send extra documents if asked. Proof of address, proof of payment method or a selfie with ID may be requested at this stage.
  7. Wait for approval. A notification confirms the result, and withdrawals become available once the check is approved.

Route A: Aadhaar Number + SMS

Photo Tips for Faster Approval

A blurry or cropped photo sends the file back for a second round. A few habits help the first upload pass the manual review:

  • Whole document in frame. All four corners visible, nothing cropped or covered by fingers.
  • Even light, no glare. Laminated Aadhaar and PAN cards reflect a flash, so daylight near a window works better.
  • Straight angle. Hold the phone flat above the card instead of shooting from the side.
  • Readable text. Zoom in before sending: name, date of birth and document number should be sharp.
  • Original, unedited. Photograph the physical card rather than a photocopy or a screenshot, and skip filters or cropping apps.
  • Both sides where printed. Aadhaar, driving licence and Voter ID carry details on the back, so add the reverse side when the form asks for a second image.
  • Selfie with ID. Face and document both in focus, with the card held next to the face, not in front of the face.

Protect the account. Parimatch support does not ask for passwords or OTP codes and does not start conversations on WhatsApp. The Aadhaar SMS code goes only into the Account Confirmation form, and documents are uploaded only there, never sent to a chat contact.

KYC and Parimatch Withdrawals

Passing KYC is the main condition for a payout, but the cashier checks a few more points before releasing money:

  • phone number and e-mail confirmed in the profile;
  • withdrawal sent to the same method used for deposits, where that method supports payouts;
  • UPI ID or bank account registered in the account holder's name;
  • wagering of any active bonus finished;
  • the deposit usually wagered at least once (1x) before cashing out.

FAQ About Parimatch Verification

Can I bet on Parimatch without verification?

Yes. Registration, deposits and bets are available straight away. KYC is required only before withdrawing money, including bonus winnings.

Which document gives the fastest Parimatch KYC?

Aadhaar. Entering the 12-digit number and the SMS code sent to the Aadhaar-linked phone can be quicker than a manual review of an uploaded photo.

How long does Parimatch verification take?

Usually a few hours, up to 24 hours. In busy periods the review can take up to 48 hours; if the status is still pending after that, the 24/7 Online Chat can check the request.

Is there an amount that triggers KYC?

Some reports point to an automatic check once a withdrawal goes above about USD 100, or roughly ₹7,500–₹8,500. The threshold may differ from account to account, so the simplest plan is to verify before the first cash-out.

Is a selfie needed for Parimatch verification?

Not by default. A selfie with ID, proof of address or proof of payment method is requested only for some accounts, for example after large or unusual transactions.

Can the Aadhaar SMS go to a new number?

No, the code goes to the phone linked to Aadhaar. If that number is no longer in use, upload a photo of Aadhaar, PAN or another accepted ID instead.
