What Is Aviator at Parimatch

Aviator is a crash game made by Spribe and released in 2019. Instead of reels or cards, the screen shows a small plane taking off while a multiplier climbs next to the flight path. The round can end at any moment: the plane flies away, and every bet still in play is lost.

A player who taps Cash Out before the plane leaves receives the stake multiplied by the value on screen: a ₹200 bet cashed out at 1.5x returns ₹300, while the same bet left running until the plane flies away returns nothing. Waiting longer raises both the possible payout and the chance of losing the stake, and each round is over in a matter of seconds.

Two Bets, Auto Bet and Auto Cash Out

Aviator shows two bet panels side by side, each with a separate stake and cash-out moment. Some players cash out one bet early and leave the second running for a higher multiplier. This splits the risk inside a round but puts more money on the table, so the combined stake must fit the session budget.

Auto Bet repeats the same stake at the start of every round until switched off. Auto Cash Out closes the bet automatically once the multiplier reaches a target set in advance. Neither feature changes the odds: if the plane leaves before the target, the stake is lost as with a manual bet.

Set Up Auto Bet

  1. Open the Auto tab on one of the bet panels.
  2. Enter the stake.
  3. Turn on Auto Bet; switch off to stop.

Set Up Auto Cash Out

  1. Open the Auto tab on the bet panel.
  2. Turn on Auto Cash Out.
  3. Enter the target multiplier.
  4. Place the bet as usual.

How Provably Fair Works in Aviator

With Provably Fair technology, the result of each Aviator round can be checked rather than taken on trust. The point where the plane flies away is calculated from:

  • The server seed generated by the game for the round.
  • The client seeds of the first three players who place bets in that round.

Because part of the input comes from players, the result is not set by the server alone.

How to Check a Round

  1. Open the round history in Aviator.
  2. Select a finished round to see the server seed, the three client seeds and the result.
  3. Use the in-game check to confirm that these seeds produce the multiplier recorded for that round.

Provably Fair proves that a past round was not altered. No tool can read the next multiplier before the bets are in.

Aviator Strategy: Bankroll Management Ideas

No Aviator strategy beats a 97% RTP game over time, and no pattern changes the next round. A plan can only control how fast money is spent and when a session ends.

Budget

Fix the Session Budget First

Decide the amount before opening the game. When the budget is gone, the session is over.

Stakes

Keep Stakes Flat

A steady stake, even the ₹10 minimum, stretches a budget over more rounds. Raising the stake after a loss only speeds up losses.

Targets

Know the Trade-Off of Targets

A low Auto Cash Out target pays more often but in small amounts; a high target pays rarely. Neither changes the long-run return.

Time

Set a Time Limit

Short rounds add up fast, so an hour of play means a long run of bets. Use a timer or a session reminder.

Martingale, doubling the stake after every loss, fails once a short losing streak pushes the stake past the budget or the maximum bet of around ₹8,000. Chasing losses with bigger bets is the fastest way to empty a balance.
