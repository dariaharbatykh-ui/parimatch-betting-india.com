Outright Bets on Tournament Winners

Before a big event, outright markets let a bettor back one team to win the whole tournament. Such a bet is settled only after the final, so the stake stays tied up for the length of the event. Odds on favourites and outsiders shift as the group stage and playoffs go on.

Dota 2 Betting Tips: Patches, Drafts and Rosters

No method removes risk, but a few habits make Dota 2 odds easier to read.

Follow the Patch

Major patches change which heroes and strategies are strong. Some teams adapt quickly, others need a whole event, so results from before a big patch say less.

Read the Draft

Line-ups built for early fights tend to mean more kills and shorter maps; late-game drafts point the other way. Kills totals and map duration follow from this.

Check Rosters and Stand-Ins

A stand-in player can weaken a strong team for a whole tournament. Confirm the line-up before betting on reputation.

Mind the Series Format

Longer series give the stronger team room to recover from a bad map. Short series leave more room for upsets, so smaller stakes make sense there.
